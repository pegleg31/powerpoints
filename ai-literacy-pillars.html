The Six Pillars of
AI Literacy

SNHU's AI Literacy Framework is built upon six foundational pillars that guide ethical, effective, and responsible use of artificial intelligence across our community. Each pillar represents a key competency and value that supports our vision for AI literacy.

1

Purposeful Use

Determine when AI is appropriate, what role it should play, and when it should not be used.

3

5

Safe and Compliant Use

Growth and Adaptation

Build transferable skills that evolve as tools and expectations change.

These pillars remain constant throughout the journey to AI literacy, providing a structured foundation for learning, practicing, and sustaining responsible AI use at SNHU.
